# Launch posts

Drafts to post once the site is live at https://playballparkgame.com. Read each community's rules first: most allow one post about your own game, and some only on certain days.

Post one a day rather than all at once, so you can answer comments on each.

## Reddit: r/WebGames

**Title:** Ballpark: guess the price of five things a day, from a single pea to a sports car

**Body:**
I made a free daily game. You get five things to price, from pocket change up to life savings, and you're scored on how close you get in ratio, so being 2× off on a paperclip costs the same as 2× off on a Porsche.

No sign-up and no app. It takes about two minutes, and you can see how you did against everyone else when you finish.

https://playballparkgame.com

I'd like to hear which prices feel wrong and what you'd want to price next.

## Reddit: r/dailygames

**Title:** Ballpark, a daily price-guessing game (five items, about two minutes)

**Body:**
Every day everyone gets the same five things to price: one cheap, one everyday, one splurge, one big purchase and one enormous. You type a price or drag a slider, see the real price, and get up to 100 points per item.

It keeps a streak, has the last 30 days to replay, and gives you a result to share.

https://playballparkgame.com

## Hacker News

**Title:** Show HN: Ballpark – a daily game where you guess what things cost

**Body:**
Five items a day, spanning roughly eight orders of magnitude (a dried pea to a hypercar). Scoring is on log error, so 2× off is 70 points whatever the item costs, and 10× off is zero.

It's plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no build step, hosted on Cloudflare Pages with a small D1 table for anonymous results. The server works out scores itself from the item list. Until 100 people have played a day, the comparison is padded with clearly labeled simulated players.

The thing I found most interesting while building it: people overprice tiny things and underprice huge ones.

https://playballparkgame.com
