Privacy policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026. Contact: dombrain.griffin@gmail.com.

This is a starting draft. Have it reviewed before launch, and update it whenever you add ads or analytics.

What stays in your browser

Your guesses, scores and stats are saved in your browser’s local storage so you can come back to them. They never leave your device unless you finish a day (see below). Clearing your browser data deletes them.

What we receive when you finish a day

When you finish a day, the game sends that day’s date, the five items, your five guesses, your score and a random ID created by your browser. The ID lets us keep one result per browser per day. It isn’t linked to your name, email or account, because Ballpark has no accounts. We use these results only to show players how they compare.

Our host, Cloudflare, processes standard request data such as IP addresses to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. We don’t store IP addresses with your results.

Third parties

Fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when it serves them.

Children

Ballpark isn’t directed at children under 13 and doesn’t knowingly collect their personal information.

Your choices

You can play without sending results by clearing your browser data before finishing, or ask us to delete a result by emailing us the random ID shown in your browser’s local storage under ballpark:player.
